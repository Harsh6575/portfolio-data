A full-stack SaaS platform for tailor and garment shop business automation,
built to replace spreadsheet-driven workflows with a proper order-to-invoice
pipeline. The Next.js frontend talks to a secure Express backend through a
type-safe Drizzle ORM layer, keeping customer measurements, order lifecycles,
inventory levels, and billing all in sync in real time.

## Key Highlights

- Customer order & measurement tracking
- Real-time inventory management
- Billing & invoice generation
- RESTful API architecture
- JWT authentication & role-based access
- Responsive modern UI design
